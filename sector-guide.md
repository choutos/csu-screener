# Sector Selection Guide

*Which verticals should you develop deep expertise in?*

---

## The CSU Principle

CSU doesn't invest in sectors — they invest in **business models**. But they've accumulated deep expertise in vertical software because that's where their model works. Your job is the same: find the intersection of:

1. **Where you have (or can build) an informational edge**
2. **Where the CSU business model applies** (sticky, low-cost-to-customer, mission-critical)
3. **Where public market inefficiency is highest** (too small for institutions, too boring for growth funds)

## How to Choose Your "1-2 Sectors"

**Step 1: Start with what you know.**
Your biggest edge is domain expertise. You spend 8+ hours/day in telecom infrastructure. That's knowledge no fund analyst has.

**Step 2: Map the vertical.**
For telecom specifically:
- What software does a telco *have to* run? (Billing, provisioning, number portability, E911, regulatory compliance)
- Which of those are public companies?
- Which are private but have public competitors?
- What % of a telco's costs is software? (Likely <1%)

**Step 3: Build the database.**
Start tracking every company in your chosen vertical. After 6 months, you'll know more than 95% of analysts covering that space.

**Step 4: Talk to people.**
CSU's edge is talking to founders for 7-9 years before buying. Your equivalent: talk to people in the industry. Your Telnyx colleagues, industry forums, conferences, LinkedIn.
